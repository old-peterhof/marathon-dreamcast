1. **Sky.** The landscape texture renders correctly underneath (planet and
   horizon visible in `~/Downloads/IMG_2659.HEIC`) with a pattern of fine

   diagnosis (a non-resident texture bound; that fix is still right and
   stays) and the stripes remained on b84. Ruled out since: GLdc's twiddle
   layout for a 1024x512 texture is bit-identical to KOS's
   `pvr_txr_load_ex` square-block layout, so the bitmap is not scrambled.
   Best remaining hypothesis: other world polygons drawn over the sky.
   `RenderAsLandscape` (OGL_Render.cpp) draws the sky in the opaque list with
   `glDepthMask(GL_FALSE)` and vertex z = -1 under the screen projection (the
   b77 fix for black holes where walls lost the depth test against a near
   depth the sky had written). Geometry beyond a landscaped transparent line
   is still in the render tree; with the sky writing no depth it passes
   LEQUAL and paints over the sky. Flycast and the PowerVR may not agree on
   "z write disable" for opaque polygons, which would make it hardware-only.
   The green corrugated wall texture of Waterloo is what the stripes look
   like. Test: give the sky the far depth instead of masking writes (z at the
   far plane, `glDepthMask` left on) so anything behind fails and anything in
   front passes, then check "Moon"/"Outer Space" do not regress to black
   holes. Note `glDepthRange(1,0)` at OGL_Render.cpp:522 inverts depth;
   whether GLdc honours glDepthRange at all is worth one look.

## Later in the night (in progress; each item is its own commit)

- **Level load: 26 s + 14 s -> under a second.** `d0f96f2`. KOS's ISO9660
  driver serves any read that is not sector-aligned into a 32-byte-aligned
  buffer one sector per GD-ROM command; stdio's 1 KB buffer guaranteed that.
  `dc_read_file_span()` reads a collection or a sound in one aligned transfer.
  Measured: collections 25945 -> 480 ms, monster sounds 13712 -> 10 ms.
- **Screen flashes were opaque white.** Root cause is in GLdc: with no colour
  array enabled, its generic `glDrawArrays` path fills every vertex white and
  ignores `glColor*`. Only the fast path (3-float positions) and immediate mode
  honoured the current colour. Aleph One's sprites and walls use GL_DOUBLE
  positions and the fader quad uses 2-float positions, so none hit the fast
  path: fades drew white and opaque, and sprites and unsplit walls drew unlit.
  Fixed in GLdc (`attributes.c`, `_fillCurrentColourARGB`), recorded as item 4
  in `tools/patches/gldc-1.1.1-fixes.patch`, backups in
  `/opt/toolchains/dc/kos-ports/libGL/.claude-backup-2026-09-06/`. libGL
  rebuilt (needs `source /opt/toolchains/dc/kos/environ.sh` first, or kos-cc
  fails with `exec: --: invalid option`).
- **Teleport static is real static now.** `TextureManager::StaticNoise`: for
  `_static_transfer` the sprite's texture is rebuilt every frame at quarter
  resolution with noise in the opaque texels, dissolving by `transfer_data`
  the way the desktop stipple did. The PowerVR has no logic ops or stipple, so
  the flicker lives in the texels. Verified visually with the STATICTEST hook
  (every sprite static): correct silhouettes of noise.
- **glEnable/glDisable filter** in `dc_gl_compat.h`: unknown capabilities are
  dropped and named once instead of leaving GL_INVALID_VALUE in glGetError
  every frame.
- Test hooks, emulator only: `FADETEST` (a different fade each second) and
  `STATICTEST` (everything teleports) markers, staged by
  `make replay-test FADETEST=1 STATICTEST=1 REPLAY_SUFFIX=-fx`.

## Heap over a session

Live heap after the first load of a level is 10.5 MB (level 28) to 11.4 MB
(Waterloo). After a death and reload it is 650-900 KB higher. That is the sound
cache filling towards its 3 MB budget (`sound: N evictions, holding X of
3072 KB`), not a leak: it is capped. Worst case is therefore about 11.4 + 3 MB
live, plus a save's 0.8 MB transient, against roughly 14.5 MB usable. Tight but
bounded. If a level ever comes up short, the sound budget is the knob.
