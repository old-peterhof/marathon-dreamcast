The Marathon symbol, four placements

Icon and wordmark halo now share one amber, #FFC000 — the glow's own colour — and the selection highlight has been moved onto it too. One warm colour, used for the brand mark and for “you are here”, nothing else.

Size floor. The mark is negative space — the ring gap is 5.03% of its width. Below about 60px that gap falls under 3px and a CRT closes it, leaving a lozenge. Rules the icon out as a row cursor — rows are 34px.
