The real wordmark, four treatments

The SVG has four stacked layers: a blurred amber bloom (raster), solid amber letterforms, black letterforms inset 2px on top of them, and DURANDAL in grey. I have split them apart so bloom, halo and face can each be driven independently. Controls below drive tiles 2–4; tile 1 is fixed as the reference.

Bloom Halo

16-bit banding. The bloom is a long shallow amber ramp and the framebuffer has ~32 steps per channel to spend on it, so on a real TV it will ring. It is baked into the static plate, so dithering it once at bake time costs nothing at runtime. Radius under ~0.8 here is short enough to get away with undithered.
